#### 15:58 - Brainstorm: Minister (no issue yet, by his word)
anwarachilles: Minister was first meant to maintain Rune (security above all), but leans toward **communication between runites and PHP** — a scheme he had in mind and never had time for. **Two-way** if possible, with Specter's help; the right scenario is still exploration. His facts — Rune behaves oddly toward other PHP, not a design flaw but because Rune stands **above** PHP files, a mediator between them: (1) Rune is itself PHP; (2) what Crafter makes is PHP; (3) the devserver serves the PHP beneath, not the runite; (4) another PHP file run from the CLI can `require` a runite.
My view: three directions — *down* (PHP the runite makes or serves, which does not know it), *up* (PHP that calls a runite), *across* (separate processes talking: Specter). The root of the oddness: a runite is a **program, not a library** (flow, argv, footer), and Essence is `$GLOBALS`, Entity global functions — two runites, or a runite inside another app, share one global space and may clash in Rune versions; so *up* is safest out of process, in process only on the same Rune and knowingly. Minister as protocoler granting an **audience**: the runite declares which casts may be called from outside, a fixed message shape (cast, spells → structured answer), and the security he first meant (a message sealed with Cipher, casts outside the list refused); the Phantasm/codex as the self-describing list. Two-way without a server: a runite as a service (`Specter::loop`, detach) with **Keeper items as a mailbox** (now under lock), a socket or local HTTP later behind the same protocol. Open question to him: does the other PHP have Rune installed (`Minister::call(...)`) or is it plain PHP (a one-file client without Rune, perhaps a relic)?
