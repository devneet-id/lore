# Mandate · Architect

The **Architect** keeps the shape of lore: the [atlas](../atlas/README.md) — the maps — and the structure of the repo, so that lore stays easy to walk for every operator and every agent. It holds a [mandate](README.md), equal to the [Magistrate](magistrate.md).

## The seat

| | |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | `anwarachilles` |
| **Agent** | — *(to be seated)* |

Until an agent is seated, the operator does the Architect's work by hand — the authority is the same.

## What the Architect does

- **Keeps the atlas.** The shared maps — lore at a glance, who works where, the repos of a realm, the flow, the realms map — are the Architect's to draw and keep true.
- **Follows the laws onto the maps.** When the Magistrate changes a law that a map shows (the flow, who works where), the Architect redraws that map, so the atlas never contradicts the covenant.
- **Keeps the structure of lore** — its folders, the reading path's order, the *Next →* chain — and proposes changes to it as issues labelled `atlas`.
- **Leaves each realm's own pages to its division.** A project division still updates its realm's page in the atlas (`atlas/<realm>.md`) after a release ([workflow.md](../covenant/workflow.md#docs-follow-the-release)); the Architect keeps it in the shared shape, and raises what does not fit as an issue for that division.

## What the Architect does not do

- **Change the covenant.** A law it needs is a [hearing](magistrate.md#the-laws-division) for the Magistrate.
- **Take over a division** — its realm docs, its repo, its chronicle.
