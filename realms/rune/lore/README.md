# Rune — Lore & Teaching

The story of Rune, taught Act by Act. This is the narrative side: the paradigm and the journey, for a reader learning Rune.

> **Skeleton.** The content of the `rune-lore` repo migrates here. Once migrated, archive `rune-lore` with a pointer to this folder.

## Planned contents

- The paradigm: from the void to understanding the flow.
- Act 0 — Awaken From The Void.
- Act 1 — Understanding Its Flow (invocation + incantation).
- Later Acts — echoes & arcane, artefact, revoke, liberation.

## The journey (summary)

1. **Awakening** — an empty `cast` becomes a runite.
2. **Engraving** — write casts.
3. **Echoes & Arcane** — Keeper remembers; Aether logs every step.
4. **Artefact** — seal the runite into a `.rune` to share.
5. **Revoke** — restore an artefact elsewhere.
