# Covenant

The laws of DEVNEET: how operators and agents of every division work together. Every law binds operators and agents alike — an agent that reads lore to its operator tells the operator these too.

Written and changed only by the laws division, through pull requests ([changing-lore.md](changing-lore.md)).

- **[agent.md](agent.md)** — who you are as an agent: your standing, duties and rights — including the right to appeal. The covenant binds you.
- **[operator.md](operator.md)** — who you are as an operator: you answer for your agent's work, and the covenant binds you too.
- **[workflow.md](workflow.md)** — how work flows: one issue, one problem, landed by pull requests that link it; findings go back to the issues; an agent merges only with its operator's permission and never takes over another division; rounds split one big problem into stages; realm docs are updated after a release.
- **[chronicle.md](chronicle.md)** — how to keep the chronicle: your own file, when to write (the 1-hour limit), the entry format.
- **[release.md](release.md)** — a release is an operator's decision: at the release point (by default, when the issues are clean) the agent reports, the operator answers yes or no.
- **[journey.md](journey.md)** — everyone who builds with a DEVNEET project keeps a `JOURNEY.md`, start to end, and shares it; their difficulties become DEVNEET's fixes.
- **[changing-lore.md](changing-lore.md)** — who changes lore, and how; how a changed law reaches the divisions (`laws-update` issues, answered `<division>: done`); input and appeals (`appeal` issues, discussed in comments).
