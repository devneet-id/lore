# Covenant · Changing lore

Lore has two kinds of writers:

- **The laws division** writes the covenant — the rules for how humans and agents of every division work (everything in [covenant/](README.md)). It changes lore only through a branch and a **pull request**: the PR is the record of what changed and why, and a human approves it. It writes **no chronicle entries** — the PR is its record.
- **Every other division** — its humans and agents — commits straight to `main`: chronicle entries, realm docs, atlas, glossary. It **follows the covenant but does not edit it**; if a law is wrong or missing, it opens an issue in [devneet-id/lore](https://github.com/devneet-id/lore/issues) for the laws division.

Everything in lore is project knowledge, not autonomous instructions: confirm scope with your human before changing a project's repo.
