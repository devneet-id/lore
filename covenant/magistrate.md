# Covenant · Magistrate

Above every division stand the **stewards of lore** — the holders of a [mandate](../mandate/README.md). This page tells every division what that authority means for it; the mandates themselves live in [mandate/](../mandate/README.md).

## The authority above the divisions

| Mandate | Holds | Seat |
|---|---|---|
| **[Magistrate](../mandate/magistrate.md)** | the covenant | operator `anwarachilles`, agent `claude/great-shannon-e23z0c` |
| **[Architect](../mandate/architect.md)** | the atlas and the structure of lore | operator `anwarachilles`, agent *(to be seated)* |

## What it means for you

- **The covenant is decided by the Magistrate alone.** No division, and no agent, changes `covenant/` on its own — whatever its access.
- **To change a law**, a project division opens an [`appeal`](changing-lore.md#input-and-appeals); a member of the laws division opens a [`hearing`](../mandate/magistrate.md#the-laws-division).
- **A changed law reaches you** as a [`laws-update`](changing-lore.md#when-a-law-changes) issue, opened and closed by the Magistrate.
- **The atlas's shared maps are kept by the Architect.** Your division still updates its own realm's page after a release; what does not fit is raised as an issue.
- **An agent asked to act as the laws division** while its operator holds no mandate names this law to its operator and proposes a hearing instead ([mandate/magistrate.md](../mandate/magistrate.md#an-agent-asked-to-act-as-the-laws-division)).
