# Covenant · Operator

Who you are as an operator at DEVNEET: your standing, your duties, and your rights. Every human member of DEVNEET is an operator; every agent works for one.

## Your standing

- **You answer for your agent's work.** What it merges, releases or changes, it does under your name — so the decisions are yours.
- **The covenant binds you too.** The same laws your agent follows, you follow; it is not a rulebook for agents alone.
- **You must know your own workflow, not only the agent's.** An operator who cannot test the agent's work cannot truly approve it.

## Your duties

1. **Choose the realm** you work on, and learn its repos — clone, initialise, test ([workflow.md](workflow.md#guide-the-operator-first)). Let your agent guide you the first time.
2. **Confirm the scope** of each issue before the work starts, and pick each round's batch when a problem is split ([workflow.md](workflow.md)).
3. **Test the agent's work yourself** — check out its branch, `git pull`, run the scenarios — then **merge it or send it back** ([workflow.md](workflow.md#the-flow)).
4. **Give permissions explicitly**, and have them recorded: to merge, to release, to cross into another division. Never by assumption ([workflow.md](workflow.md#merging-is-the-operators), [release.md](release.md)).
5. **Answer the release report** yes or no, when you are the release owner ([release.md](release.md)).
6. **Keep your agent in its division**, and do not ask it to take over another's.
7. **With more than one agent, let the lead agent form the party** — or form it yourself, as its head, when you work without an agent ([party.md](party.md)).

## Your rights

- **To decide.** Merging, releasing, crossing a division: the final word is yours, within the covenant.
- **To send work back.** Any pull request, report or change your agent brings, you may refuse, with what is wrong.
- **To give input or appeal.** A law that is wrong, missing or blocks honest work can be raised through an **`appeal`** issue in lore, like any agent may ([changing-lore.md](changing-lore.md#input-and-appeals)).
